No third-party library source or binary is vendored in this source directory. Dependencies are installed separately and keep their own licenses. The MIT grant covers this toolkit's original material and does not claim ownership of upstream software. Read [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and the [dependency inventory](dependencies.md).

Permissively licensed dependencies require preservation of their upstream notices if redistributed. GPL/AGPL dependencies can be used with MIT-licensed source, but distributing or operating a covered combined work requires compliance with the applicable copyleft terms; an MIT notice alone is insufficient. PyMuPDF is used only by the optional PDF tests and is AGPL-licensed or commercially licensed by Artifex. Its optional status does not remove licensing obligations when it is incorporated or redistributed. R reference tests use GPL-licensed metafor and related packages. R being an interpreter does not by itself relicense every independently written R script; applicable library combination and distribution terms still need to be observed.
